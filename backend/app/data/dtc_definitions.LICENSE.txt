DTC codes and descriptions: the SAE-controlled (generic) ranges of
https://github.com/Wal33D/dtc-database at 04c43d72e7db7197658b6f72fe582c5076d9eee8
(MIT, below), checked against https://github.com/lennykean/OBDII.DTC at

backend/tools/build_dtc_definitions.py. The category, subcategory, severity,
